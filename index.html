MetLife.com Login – Access Your MetLife Account

Learn how to sign in, register, recover your account, and access available MetLife insurance and benefits services online.

Visit MetLife

MetLife.com Login provides eligible customers and employees with online access to their MetLife account and available insurance or benefits information. Depending on the type of account, users may be able to review coverage details, access benefit information, check claims, view documents, and manage certain account services.

This guide explains the general MetLife login process and provides helpful information about registration, password recovery, and common login problems.

Security Tip: Always verify that you are using the official MetLife website before entering your username, password, or other personal account information.

How to Use MetLife.com Login

Follow these general steps to access your MetLife account online:

1
Open the official MetLife website.
2
Select the Log In or account sign-in option.
3
Choose the appropriate account or benefit service if prompted.
4
Enter your registered username, email address, or requested login information.
5
Enter your password carefully.
6
Select Log In to continue.
7
After successful authentication, access the account services available to you.

What Can You Do After MetLife Login?

The services available after signing in depend on your MetLife account and benefits plan. Depending on eligibility, your online account may allow you to:

  • Review insurance and benefit information
  • View available plan details
  • Check claims and claim-related information
  • Access account documents
  • Review coverage details
  • Manage certain account preferences
  • Find information about available benefits

Forgot Your MetLife Login Information?

If you cannot remember your username or password, use the account recovery or password-help option provided on the official MetLife login page.

Follow the instructions displayed on the screen to verify your account and recover or reset your login information. Never share your password or security verification codes with other people.

How to Register for a MetLife Account

If you are using MetLife online for the first time, you may need to create an account before signing in.

  1. Visit the official MetLife website.
  2. Select the available registration option.
  3. Provide the information requested to identify your account.
  4. Create your login credentials.
  5. Complete any required verification steps.
  6. Return to the login page and sign in.

Registration requirements can vary depending on the MetLife service or benefits account you are trying to access.

MetLife Login Not Working?

If your MetLife.com Login is not working, try these basic troubleshooting steps:

  • Check that your username or email address is correct.
  • Re-enter your password carefully.
  • Make sure Caps Lock is turned off.
  • Use the password recovery option if necessary.
  • Refresh the login page.
  • Try another supported web browser.
  • Clear your browser cache and cookies.
  • Check your internet connection.
  • Use official MetLife support options if the problem continues.

Is MetLife.com Login Secure?

Online account security is important when accessing insurance and benefits information. Users should keep their login credentials private, create strong passwords, avoid suspicious links, and sign out when using a shared computer.

Never provide your password or security verification codes through unsolicited emails, messages, or social media.

Frequently Asked Questions

MetLife.com Login is the online sign-in process used by eligible users to access their MetLife accounts and available services.

Can I check my benefits after logging in?

Depending on your account and plan, you may be able to view benefits, coverage details, documents, and other account information.

Use the password recovery or account-help option available through the official MetLife login service and follow the instructions provided.

Why can't I log in to my MetLife account?
